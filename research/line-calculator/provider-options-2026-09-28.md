# Alternative odds-source screening — 28 September 2026

Purpose: find a licensed source of many **simultaneous, paired over/under lines** from the same event, bookmaker, market definition and timestamp, with historical depth and results. In particular, a `throw-ins` label in a catalogue does not prove that 38.5, 39.5, 40.5, etc. are actually quoted together for enough soccer matches. This is source discovery, not model approval.

## Ranked acquisition path

1. **LSports trial or historical sample**, specifying the exact laterals question and a second set of common markets. Ask for at least 100 recent settled soccer fixtures across several leagues/books first, with all simultaneous alternate lines, both sides, capture time, pre/live flag, precise settlement rules and final counts. If yield is real, price an export sized for thousands of independent events and written rights to store derived model parameters and serve estimates. An arbitrary 100 event sample only screens yield; it does not validate a model.
2. **SportsDataIO real historical sample** as a check on documented multi-line depth. Confirm soccer throw-ins in its market metadata before paying. Its US-book focus may limit laterals; its multiple-total structure is useful for goals/corners if those are populated.
3. **SportsGameOdds free live key**, if the owner registers one, for a current supported-market and ladder audit. Do not infer throw-in coverage from `throwIns` stat. Historical use requires Pro pricing or negotiated sample, so it is not the cheap retrospective path.
4. **TxODDS/OpticOdds samples** if the above cannot supply enough eligible throw-in data. Demand the same fixture/market-level yield evidence and exact price before purchase.
5. **Bookmaker account (Plan B)** only after provider samples fail. A login grants a view of today's board; it does not by itself provide 5k–10k historical independent settled events, full odds history or a right to bulk export. Use a user-controlled browser session, verify the bookmaker's permitted use and practical coverage, and do a small, rate-limited pilot before deciding on any larger collection. Never ask for password in chat or bypass access controls.

## Vendor sample acceptance check

For each `(sport, metric, participant scope, period, settlement, pre/live)` family: count independent settled events, bookmakers, simultaneous snapshots, snapshots with ≥2 complete O/U lines, actual bracket widths, missing results and any line/side mismatches. Report yield by league and bookmaker, not just an overall row count. Reject a source for the intended family if it only supplies single lines, one-sided props or time-series changes in place of simultaneous neighboring lines. Purchase decisions should be based on this table plus contractual commercial/derived-data rights, not a 550-market or 5M-fixture headline.
